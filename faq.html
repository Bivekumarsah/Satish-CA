Clear answers before work begins.
What kinds of clients can the firm support?

Individuals, professionals, startups, small and medium businesses, family businesses and growing companies can all be supported depending on the actual scope and expertise of the final practice.

Can I engage the firm only for tax work?

Yes. Services can be project-based, recurring or limited to one area such as tax, bookkeeping, compliance or advisory.

What should I bring to the first consultation?

Bring your main question, relevant registrations, prior returns, financial statements, accounting records, notices, deadlines and any documents connected to the issue.

Can you clean up incomplete accounting records?

A cleanup engagement can be scoped after reviewing the records, missing periods, bank reconciliations and supporting documents.

Do you support online consultations?

The demo site supports office, phone and online meeting preferences. Replace this with the real practice policy later.

How are fees determined?

Fees normally depend on scope, complexity, transaction volume, urgency and whether work is recurring or project-based. Final pricing should be agreed before work begins.

Is information submitted on this demo website stored?

No backend is connected in this demo. Forms prepare messages locally in the browser and WhatsApp requests open externally.
