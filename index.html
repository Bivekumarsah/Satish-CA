FAQ

Questions before we start?

Here are a few practical answers. For anything specific to your situation, use the consultation form below.

What information should I prepare for the first consultation?

Bring the question you want solved plus any relevant financial statements, tax/compliance notices, registration details or internal reports. You can begin with whatever you already have.

Can you support an existing business with messy records?

Yes. The first step is usually a structured review to identify missing records, reconciliation gaps, compliance deadlines and the right cleanup sequence.

Do you work with startups and small businesses?

Yes. Early-stage businesses often benefit most from getting bookkeeping, reporting routines, controls and compliance organized before complexity grows.

Can the engagement be ongoing?

Yes. Depending on your needs, support can be project-based or structured as recurring accounting, reporting, compliance and advisory work.
